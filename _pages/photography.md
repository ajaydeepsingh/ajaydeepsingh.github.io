Photography is one of the ways I pay closer attention to the world around me. I enjoy finding interesting light, quiet details, and everyday moments that are easy to miss when moving too quickly.

I’m drawn to photographs that feel honest and unforced. I like experimenting with composition, looking for character in familiar places, and letting a scene speak for itself rather than overworking it.
